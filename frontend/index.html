Bring your Binance tier with you.

Prove your VIP level and 30-day volume once. Any protocol that reads Apostille can give you the fees you've already earned. Your Binance user ID stays hidden.

What goes on-chain

Connect a wallet on BNB Smart Chain testnet to start.

  1. Approve the request Apostille signs a request that hashes your Binance user ID and sets what gets revealed.
  2. Prove it on Binance The Primus extension opens Binance and reads your fee summary over zkTLS.
  3. Seal it on-chain Confirm one transaction. The contract checks the proof and records your tier.

You'll need the Primus extension, a Binance login in this browser, and a little testnet BNB.

Where your volume lands

You get the highest tier where your VIP level or your 30-day futures volume clears the bar. Seals last 30 days, one volume window.
